Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Welcome to Pixel Beads Pattern Generator ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

Our service is designed to process images directly in your browser. We strive to collect as little personal data as possible:

  • Images you upload: All image processing (pixelation, color reduction, bead counting) happens locally in your browser. Your uploaded images are not sent to or stored on our servers.
  • Usage data: We may collect anonymous usage statistics such as page views, browser type, and approximate geographic location (country level) through analytics tools.
  • Language preference: Your selected language is stored locally in your browser's localStorage and is never transmitted.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage to remember your language preference. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes. Third-party services (such as Google AdSense or analytics providers) may place cookies on your device according to their own privacy policies.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To improve and optimize the website experience
  • To understand aggregate usage patterns
  • To display relevant advertisements (through third-party ad networks)

4. Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense: Displays advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. See Google's advertising policy.
  • Google Analytics (or similar): Collects anonymous traffic data to help us improve the site.
  • Payment processors: If you purchase a download, your payment is processed by a third-party provider (e.g., Stripe, PayPal). We do not store your payment details.

5. Data Security

Since image processing occurs locally in your browser, your uploaded images never leave your device. We implement reasonable security measures to protect any limited data we may collect.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Disable cookies through your browser settings

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since image processing is local, no personal data from minors is transmitted to our servers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@pixelbeads.example.com
